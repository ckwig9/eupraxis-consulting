Outgrow Selling System

How One Tiny Habit Turns Team Chaos Into Predictable Revenue

Craig Wigginton · Apr 24, 2025 · 2 min read

The Overlooked Sales Discipline That Quietly Transforms Growth-Minded Teams

In a world chasing sales hacks, tools, and quick wins, the real secret to consistent growth is much simpler…almost boring.

This predictable revenue model isn’t about clever pitches or closing techniques.
It’s about consistency.

At Eupraxis Consulting, we’ve worked with growth-focused companies across industries and seen how simple, proactive actions – done daily – change everything.
This is the heart of the Outgrow framework: a small, trackable habit that creates profitable, predictable revenue over time.

Let’s unpack why this works, and why most teams miss it.

Most Sales Teams Are Busy, But Not Proactive

More than 90 percent of salespeople operate in reaction mode. They spend their days answering questions, putting out fires, responding to inbound leads.

Those tasks are important. But they’re not transformational.

Top-performing sellers play a different game. They don’t just respond. They initiate. They create conversations. They build the future instead of defending the present.

And they do it by adopting one repeatable habit:
Daily Proactive Calls.
No drama. No waiting for the perfect moment. Just consistent outbound action, done on purpose.

Stephen Covey called this “putting first things first.”
In sales, that means doing the work that builds your pipeline, not just protects it.

Forget Talent. Build Traction.

There’s a myth that great sales performance comes from personality, persuasion, or natural talent.

It doesn’t.

As Simon Sinek says, “Consistency is more important than intensity.”

Outgrow breaks with tradition here. Instead of coaching closing techniques, we help teams install a daily rhythm built around Proactive Communication.
It’s not complicated. But it’s powerful:

  • A few intentional calls every day
  • A protected time block on the calendar
  • A visible, simple system to track effort

When those three elements are in place, the chaos starts to settle.
Sales becomes focused. Predictable. Scalable.

It compounds.

The Real Power Is in the Simplicity

The beauty of the Outgrow system is that it’s built for real salespeople in real environments.
It doesn’t demand major overhauls. It starts with small shifts.

  • One more outbound call
  • One 15-minute block of protected time
  • One simple tracker to measure behavior, not just results

These aren’t theory. They’re habits.
And when practiced daily, they create a revenue flywheel that builds on itself.

Outgrow: A Framework That Installs Behavior, Not Just Ideas

Outgrow isn’t another sales model or communication theory.
It’s a field-tested framework designed to:

  • Eliminate reactive selling
  • Replace hesitation with action
  • Anchor daily behavior in structure, not mood

The process is straightforward.
The results are trackable.

From solo founders running their own pipeline to large teams inside EOS-led organizations, we’ve seen the same pattern emerge:
Small actions create significant movement—if done daily and measured clearly.

Your Next Step

Here’s a question to ask your team:
What would happen if every rep made three intentional outbound touches per day?

That’s 15 per week.
60 per month.
720 per year—per person.

Now multiply that across your team.

You’re not just building a pipeline. You’re building a flywheel.

And it all starts with one proactive call today.
