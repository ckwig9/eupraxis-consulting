5 Signs Your Company Is Ready for Outgrow

Craig Wigginton · May 6, 2025 · 3 min read

(Part 2/5 of the What is Outgrow Series)

Is Your Business Ready for Predictable Growth?

If you’re tired of depending on reactive sales and want to create a confident, proactive, growth-driven culture, Outgrow might be the revenue growth strategy your company needs next.

Outgrow isn’t a theoretical “sales training”. It’s a simple, scalable system that transforms company-wide behavior, leading to 15–30% revenue growth annually across hundreds of firms .

But how do you know if your company is ready to make the leap?

Let’s explore the 5 clear signs that you’re primed for Outgrow success, drawn directly from the experiences of 400+ companies who’ve been through it.

1. Your Sales and Customer Teams Are Reactive, Not Proactive

If your customer-facing staff primarily waits for inbound calls, fires to fight, or orders to take, that’s a flashing neon sign you’re ready for Outgrow’s revenue growth strategy.

As Outgrow explains, reactive companies scramble from one problem to the next, while proactive companies systematically drive growth by initiating communication — even when “nothing is wrong” .

Pro Tip: If your people aren’t regularly reaching out with proactive calls, DYKs (“Did You Know” questions), rDYKs (“Reverse Did You Know” questions), or Pivots, you’re leaving massive wallet share on the table.

2. Your Team Struggles with Confidence and Optimism

Behavior follows thinking. If your team’s default mindset is cautious, fearful, or pessimistic, their actions – and your growth – will mirror that.

Outgrow companies experience fast mindset shifts within 90 days:

  • From Fear to Confidence: Salespeople pick up the phone before problems arise.
  • From Pessimism to Optimism: They believe their help is valuable, not intrusive.

Pro Tip: A confident, optimistic team doesn’t just close more. They open more opportunities by showing up proactively.

3. Your Managers Are Hungry for Results (or Willing to Learn)

Managers are critical to Outgrow’s success.

According to Goldfayn, the team follows the manager’s energy. If managers buy in, teams thrive. If they resist, teams stall.

If your managers are:

  • Excited about leading a cultural shift
  • Open to coaching and tracking
  • Ready to model proactivity

…then your company is perfectly positioned to harness Outgrow’s full power.

4. Your Culture Values “Helping,” Not Just “Selling”

Outgrow companies believe they’re helping customers, not selling to them .

And this mindset is absolutely critical for Outgrow to stick.

When your team:

  • Views outreach as service, not disruption
  • Understands that silence actually hurts the customer
  • Feels proud to offer value before being asked

…you’ve created the fertile soil needed for Outgrow’s system to grow.

5. You’re Willing to Commit to Simple, Consistent Actions

Outgrow thrives on small, daily, compounding behaviors:

  • Proactive Calls
  • DYKs
  • rDYKs
  • Pivots to New Conversations
  • Asking about New Needs

If your leadership is willing to keep the gas pedal down permanently – even when it’s inconvenient – your company can see transformational gains.

Pro Tip: It’s not the complexity of actions that matters. It’s the consistency.
