</blockquote>

<p>So they go out and:</p>

<ul>
<li>Hire a senior sales leader or VP</li>

<li>Pay steep headhunter and onboarding fees</li>

<li>Promise the team a new era of growth</li>
</ul>

<p>But here’s what usually happens next:</p>

<ul>
<li>The new hire spends months “learning the business”</li>

<li>The team gets confused about who’s driving growth</li>

<li>The owner ends up still carrying the load</li>

<li>The leader struggles without a clear system</li>

<li>And sales? They stay flat</li>
</ul>

<p>Six to twelve months later, the “hero” is gone.</p>

<p>The team is still stuck.</p>

<p>And leadership is even more hesitant to invest again.</p>

<h2>Trust Your Team? Equip Them.</h2>

<p>If you find yourself saying:</p>

<ul>
<li>“My team’s capable, but not proactive”</li>

<li>“They’re good with customers, but don’t follow up”</li>

<li>“They’re busy, but growth is inconsistent”</li>
</ul>

<p>You don’t need to replace them.</p>

<p>You need to give them a system where:</p>

<li>Confidence is built through repetition, not pressure</li>

<p>When that system is in place, you’ll realize:</p>
