<p><em>(Part 2 of the <a href="business-development-culture-outgrow.html">PERMA Series</a>)</em></p>

<p>In the <a href="business-development-culture-outgrow.html">previous article</a>, we explored why most business development efforts are broken and sales are declining; not because people are lazy, but because they&#8217;re disconnected. Disconnected from customers. Disconnected from each other. Disconnected from any shared sense of purpose, rhythm, or result. At the heart of that disconnection and declining sales is something most leaders never think to measure:</p>

<p><strong>Emotion.</strong></p>

<p>More specifically: the <em>absence</em> of positive emotion in the selling process.</p>

<p>Think about it: how do your customer-facing people <em>feel</em> on a typical Tuesday?</p>

<ul>
<li>Do they feel bold…or cautious?</li>

<li>Do they feel appreciated…or overlooked?</li>

<li>Do they feel optimistic about reconnecting with customers…or anxious, like they’re bothering them?</li>
</ul>

<p>Because here’s what we know from the science of Positive Psychology: our emotions directly shape our behavior. In fact, <em>emotion precedes action.</em></p>

<p>And when your team doesn’t feel good about reaching out, they don’t do it. Period.</p>

<p>That means calls don’t get made. Leads don’t get followed up. Opportunities get “saved for later” (translation: forgotten). And an entire revenue function starts operating from fear &#8211; fear of rejection, fear of being annoying, fear of not adding value.</p>

<p>Let’s call this what it is: emotional scarcity.</p>

<p>Left unchecked, low morale quietly shows up as declining sales —&nbsp;costing your business seven or even eight figures per year.</p>

<h4>Because when positive emotion is low, sales performance isn’t just flat, it’s frozen.</h4>

<p>Your best people play it safe. Your average performers go silent. And only the natural extroverts (if you&#8217;re lucky enough to have them) carry the load. Even then, they’re often under-supported, emotionally isolated, and burning out.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, your customers? They’re not hearing from you nearly enough. And when they do, it’s reactive, like when something breaks, when a bill is due, or when it’s too late to win the work.</p>

<p>This is the <em>opposite</em> of what business development should feel like.</p>

<p>And more importantly, it’s the opposite of what it <em>could</em> feel like.</p>

<p>Now imagine this: what if your salespeople, your CSRs, even your ops folks <em>felt good</em> every time they reached out to a customer?</p>

<p>What if every proactive call or text or check-in wasn’t a burden, but a <em>confidence booster</em>?</p>

<p>What if reaching out was associated not with rejection… but with <em>reward</em>?</p>

<p>That’s the power of Positive Emotion. And it’s not just a nice-to-have. It’s a <em>growth multiplier.</em> When intentionally cultivated, it drives performance across the board.</p>

<h4>When your team believes they’re helpful, valued, and appreciated, they act accordingly.</h4>

<p>So the question becomes: how do you engineer more of <em>that</em>?</p>

<p>In the next section, we’ll break down exactly how Positive Emotion becomes the foundation of a flourishing sales culture, and what it looks like when it shows up in every corner of your business.</p>

<h2>How Positive Emotion Becomes a Revenue-Driving Force Inside Your Team</h2>

<p>Most people think sales is about persuasion.</p>

<p>I think it’s about <em>emotion</em>.</p>

<p>And when you create a culture where <strong>positive emotion</strong> is not only present but <em>normalized</em>, the impact on sales performance is transformative.</p>

<p>We’re not talking about forced enthusiasm or cheesy affirmations. We’re talking about a deep, contagious <em>sense of confidence, gratitude, and optimism</em> that fuels helpful outreach; the kind that drives revenue without pressure.</p>

<p>Here’s what it looks like in a company that has it:</p>

<ul>
<li>Sales reps pick up the phone <em>because they want to</em>, not because they were told to.</li>

<li>Service team members follow up <em>not just to fix problems</em>, but to surface new opportunities they can help with.</li>

<li>Ops leaders pass leads to sales <em>not because it&#8217;s their job</em>, but because it feels good to help move the business forward.</li>
</ul>

<p>Let me illustrate:</p>

<p>Suppose a commercial construction company was struggling with stagnant growth. The team was competent, but cautious. Their outreach was limited to quotes and complaints. One of the first things we do with Outgrow is <strong>source genuine positive feedback from their customers &#8211;</strong> the kind of comments that are usually buried in inboxes or forgotten after a call.</p>

<p>We pull these into a testimonial book and we listen to the transcripts together.</p>

<p>The effect is immediate.</p>

<p>People <em>sit up straighter.</em> I’ve heard comments like, “I didn’t realize our customers actually thought about us like that.” Or, “It feels so good to know we make that kind of difference in their world.”</p>

<h4>That’s the shift: when your team <em>knows</em> they matter, their fear of rejection shrinks, and their energy skyrockets.</h4>

<p>And the sales results? In the next 90 days, Outgrow companies often surface more net-new opportunities than the previous <em>two quarters combined.</em> Not because they learned new closing tactics, but because they finally believed they had something worth sharing.</p>

<p>That’s Positive Emotion in action.</p>

<figure><img src="assets/blog/perma-2-when-people-feel-emotionally-safe-they-become-emotionally-gene.jpg" alt="When people feel emotionally safe, they become emotionally generous" width="800" height="420" loading="lazy"></figure>

<p>When people feel <em>emotionally safe</em>, they become <strong>emotionally generous;</strong> with customers, with each other, and with themselves.</p>

<p>And this isn’t just a feel-good theory. Positive Psychology research shows that:</p>

<ul>
<li>Positive emotion expands creativity and problem-solving capacity.</li>

<li>It increases resilience and persistence.</li>

<li>And it improves relationships, all of which are essential to sustainable sales growth.</li>
</ul>

<p>So what if your company didn’t just experience this as a <em>moment</em>, but as a <em>sysftem</em>?</p>

<p>What if confidence wasn’t left to chance…but built into the way you do business?</p>

<p>That’s where we’re headed in the next section, where I’ll show you exactly how the <strong>Outgrow system activates Positive Emotion</strong> daily, across your entire customer-facing team.</p>

<p>This isn’t theoretical. It’s operational. And it’s how you turn confidence into cash flow &#8211; one positive touch at a time.</p>

<h2>Turning Confidence Into a Daily Habit: How Outgrow Systematizes Positive Emotion</h2>

<p>If positive emotion is the <em>fuel</em> of sales, then Outgrow is the <em>engine</em> that burns it efficiently; every day, across every customer-facing role.</p>

<p>We’re not hoping for morale. We’re engineering it.</p>

<p>Here’s how.</p>

<h3>Step 1: Start With the Customer’s Voice</h3>

<p>Most teams never hear how valuable they are, because we don’t create space to listen. So Outgrow begins by mining for gold: real, specific, positive feedback from your <em>existing</em> customers.</p>

<p>Think of this as the “Confidence Bank.”</p>

<p>It’s your team’s emotional reserve and a catalogue of your customers’ experience, like:</p>

<ul>
<li>Their favorite things about your company.</li>

<li>The ways in which your company helps them.</li>

<li>Stories of how specific team members make a difference.</li>
</ul>

<p>Once collected, we drip this feedback into weekly team huddles and monthly reviews. And suddenly, the tone of the room changes. You can <em>see</em> the confidence kick in.</p>

<p>Confidence is built through evidence. The most powerful source of sales courage is knowing <em>the customer already values you</em>.</p>

<h3>Step 2: Anchor Every Swing in Helpfulness</h3>

<p>Outgrow’s daily outreach &#8211; what we call “swings” &#8211; is built on emotional safety. We don’t ask your team to “pitch” or “close” or “sell harder.” Instead, we ask them to <em>help</em> more customers, more often.</p>

<p>That’s the emotional unlock.</p>

<p>Because asking “What else can I help you with?” feels natural. And to say “Just thinking about you” and “What’s coming up that we can help you with?” feels human.</p>

<p>This is where positive emotion meets action.</p>

<p>Each team member makes 3–5 simple, low-pressure actions a day:</p>

<ul>
<li>A phone call to check in on a project they helped complete.</li>

<li>A text to a warm contact: “I’m working with a client that made me think of you. How’s Tues to connect?”</li>

<li>A referral follow-up: “Just wanted to say thanks again &#8211; who else can we help the way we help you?”</li>
</ul>

<p>These aren’t “sales calls.” They’re <strong>emotional trust deposits.</strong></p>

<p>And when your people <em>feel good</em> reaching out, they start doing it more. Which leads to more conversations, more opportunities, and more wins.</p>

<h3>Step 3: Celebrate the Action, Not Just the Outcome</h3>

<p>Positive emotion dies in silence. So at the core of the Outgrow rhythm is <em>recognition</em>.</p>

<p>In every Monday Outgrow Huddle, we share wins… but not just closed deals. We celebrate:</p>

<ul>
<li>The swing that reopened a dormant customer.</li>

<li>The rep who followed up and booked a discovery call.</li>

<li>The ops teammate who surfaced an upsell just by asking, “What else are you working on?”</li>
</ul>

<p>By praising the <em>effort</em>, not just the result, we reinforce the belief: <em>“You matter. Your actions matter. Keep going.”</em></p>

<p>And because we track swings, you can see effort rising. Culture lifts. Performance follows.</p>

<h3>Step 4: Reinforce With Leadership Language</h3>

<p>This process only works if it starts from the top. That’s why we help CEOs and sales leaders speak the language of positive reinforcement. Often it’s just a simple, sincere:</p>

<ul>
<li>“Look at who’s swinging the bat! Great job team.”</li>

<li>“I saw what you did there. That was proactive.”</li>

<li>“Keep swinging. It’s working.”</li>
</ul>

<p>You’d be shocked how much momentum that creates, especially for team members who aren’t used to being seen.</p>

<p>In just minutes a day, this system turns optimism into output.</p>

<p>And when you normalize that across your organization &#8211; when your team believes they’re <em>welcomed</em> by customers instead of resented &#8211; you’ve unlocked the emotional flywheel of sustainable sales growth.</p>

<h2>If Your Team Felt This Confident Every Day…What Would Be Possible?</h2>

<p>Here’s the truth: declining sales aren’t the result of your team’s lack of strategy or skills.<br></p>

<p>You don’t need to overhaul your org chart or hire expensive closers. You already have the right people. They just need the right <em>system;</em> one that supports them emotionally, activates them daily, and gives them permission to show up with courage.</p>

<p>That’s what Outgrow does. It <em>builds confidence into the culture.</em> Not as an accident, but as a weekly habit.</p>
