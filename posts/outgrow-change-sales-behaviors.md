<h2>What Are Proactive Calls?</h2>

<p><strong>Proactive Calls</strong> are at the heart of the Outgrow system.</p>

<p>They are <strong>brief, relationship-building outreach</strong> to customers and prospects, made <strong>when nothing is wrong</strong>.</p>

<p>These calls typically follow a <strong>three-part, three-minute structure</strong>:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>The Opening:</strong> A personal, relationship-first check-in (example: &#8220;I was thinking about you. How’s the family?&#8221;)</li>

<li><strong>Shift to Business:</strong> Asking helpful, opportunity-opening questions (DYKs and rDYKs).</li>

<li><strong>Pivot to a Next Step:</strong> Moving naturally toward a quote, an order, or a future meeting .</li>
</ol>

<p>Proactive Calls aren’t pushy.</p>

<p>They’re human, professional, and confidence-building — both for the salesperson and the customer.</p>

<h2>What Are DYKs (Did You Know Questions)?</h2>

<p><strong>DYKs (&#8220;Did You Know&#8221; questions)</strong> are simple ways to inform and educate customers about additional products, services, or solutions they might not be aware you offer.</p>

<p>Examples:</p>

<ul>
<li>&#8220;Did you know we also carry product X and Y now?&#8221;</li>

<li>&#8220;Did you know we expanded our service area last month?&#8221;</li>
</ul>

<p>According to Outgrow’s 20+ years of tracking:</p>

<ul>
<li>About <strong>20% of DYKs directly create new business</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<p>The goal?</p>

<p><strong>Maximize your team’s use of DYKs,</strong> because each one is a simple swing at growing customer value.</p>

<h2>What Are rDYKs (Reverse Did You Know Questions)?</h2>

<p><strong>rDYKs (&#8220;Reverse Did You Know&#8221; questions)</strong> flip the focus.</p>

<p>Instead of telling customers something new, you ask <strong>what they need</strong> that you’re not currently providing.</p>

<p>Examples:</p>

<ul>
<li>&#8220;What else do you have going on that I can help you with?&#8221;</li>

<li>&#8220;What aren’t we quoting you today that we should be?&#8221;</li>
</ul>

<p>rDYKs help uncover hidden opportunities.</p>

<p>They show customers that you care enough to ask, not just to sell.</p>

<h2>What Are Pivots?</h2>

<p><strong>Pivots</strong> are natural invitations to move forward, either toward a sale or toward the next conversation.</p>

<p>Examples:</p>

<ul>
<li>&#8220;When would you like us to deliver this?&#8221;</li>

<li>&#8220;Can I add that to your current order?&#8221;</li>

<li>&#8220;When should I follow up with you on this?&#8221;</li>
</ul>

<p>Pivots eliminate lingering, awkward &#8220;maybes&#8221; and make it easy for customers to say yes…to the next product, the next call, or the next meeting.</p>
